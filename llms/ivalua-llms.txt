- [Abastecimiento de materiales directos](https://www.ivalua.com/glossary/direct-materials-sourcing/): El abastecimiento directo de materiales es un componente fundamental de la adquisición centrada en la adquisición eficiente de materias primas, bienes y servicios esenciales para la producción de bienes o servicios\.
- [Accounts Payable Automation \(AP Automation\)](https://www.ivalua.com/glossary/accounts-payable-automation-ap-automation/): Accounts Payable Automation, also known as AP Automation, pertains to the use of specialized software or technology designed for accounts payable management\.
- [Accounts Payable \(AP\)](https://www.ivalua.com/glossary/accounts-payable-ap/): Accounts Payable refers to the amount that a business owes to its suppliers, representing the sum owed by a business to its suppliers, is recorded as a liability on the company's balance sheet\.
